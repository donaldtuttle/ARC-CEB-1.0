# R1–R8 residual-defect classes

Closed set. There is no OTHER, MISC, or free-form class. Issues that cannot
be typed into R1–R8 are `OUT_OF_BOUNDARY`.

Public trigger-code **labels** below are taken from `docs/CASE_MATRIX.md` on
the repository main branch. Full trigger **predicates** and fixed statement
templates live in the locked protocol text. If those predicates are not
loaded, write `UNDEFINED in this document: trigger predicates`.

“Single-defect” in the 24-case suite means one **emitted candidate**, not
one trigger code. Multiple codes collapse into one candidate when they share
the same target and class.

## R1 — Support gap

Operational question: is a load-bearing claim unsupported or its evidence
unverified?

Public labels:

- `EVIDENCE_NOT_VERIFIED`
- `DEDUCTION_HAS_NO_DEPENDENCIES`

A deduction with no `depends_on` edges is a support gap even if the prose
sounds confident. Unverified evidence is still a gap if the claim is
load-bearing on the boundary.

## R2 — Untested assumption

Operational question: does the conclusion depend on an assumption without a
valid completed test?

Public labels:

- `ASSUMPTION_TEST_MISSING`
- `ASSUMPTION_TEST_UNRUN`

Missing and unrun are different codes. Do not collapse them unless they
share target and class **and** the locked generator says they collapse.

## R3 — Undefined operative term

Operational question: is a term doing logical work without a registered
definition?

Public labels:

- `DEFINITION_ID_MISSING`
- `DEFINITION_CLAIM_MISSING`

Stylistic or unused vocabulary is outside the boundary. Only terms referenced
from the closed claim set count.

## R4 — Alternative or confound gap

Operational question: are alternatives, confounds, or discriminating tests
missing or unresolved?

Public labels:

- `ALTERNATIVE_SET_EMPTY`
- `DISCRIMINATOR_MISSING`
- `DISCRIMINATOR_UNRUN`
- `DISCRIMINATOR_FAILED`

`DISCRIMINATOR_FAILED` is the unique-fatal fixture `ARC24-022`. Failure of a
discriminator is not a style note; it can invalidate the headline.

## R5 — Missing failure or rejection condition

Operational question: is a required falsifier, failure gate, or rejection
rule absent?

Public labels:

- `REQUIRED_KIND_FALSIFIER_MISSING`
- `HEADLINE_CLAIM_FALSIFIER_MISSING`

Absence of a falsifier is a class-R5 candidate. Whether that candidate
warrants `NEXT_PROMPT` is a scoring question, not an enumeration question.

## R6 — Scope-bridge gap

Operational question: does evidence from one scope support a claim in
another without a valid bridge?

Public labels:

- `SCOPE_MISMATCH_MODEL`
- additional scope-mismatch codes in the locked protocol (`ARC24-013`
  covers five scope mismatches collapsed to one candidate)

Do not invent new scope names. Use the closed `kind` / scope vocabulary of
the frozen record.

## R7 — Internal inconsistency

Operational question: does the record contain a cycle, polarity conflict,
or incompatible claim?

Public labels:

- `DEPENDENCY_CYCLE`
- `POLARITY_CONFLICT`
- `NUMERIC_INTERVAL_CONFLICT`

Cycles are structural. Polarity and numeric-interval conflicts are
content-level incompatibilities already present in the frozen record — not
new theories imagined by the enumerator.

## R8 — Operationalization, measurement, or realization gap

Operational question: is the measurement, proxy, implementation, or
realization bridge missing or invalid?

Public labels:

- `MEASUREMENT_ID_MISSING`
- `VERIFICATION_TEST_MISSING`
- `MEASUREMENT_NOT_VALIDATED`
- `PROXY_VALIDITY_BRIDGE_MISSING`
- `REALIZATION_BRIDGE_MISSING`

`REALIZATION_BRIDGE_MISSING` is the unique-fatal fixture `ARC24-023`.

## Out of boundary

Record the notice. Do not add a candidate. Do not change the terminal
action. Typical out-of-boundary material: stylistic observations, unregistered
external facts, newly imagined alternative theories, prior conversation not
in the frozen record, “interesting” side questions.
